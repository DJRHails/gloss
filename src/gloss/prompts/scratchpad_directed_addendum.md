## Your scratchpad

You have a `scratchpad` tool. Before you decide on moves, call `scratchpad` and do your
thinking there — the whole of it, not a summary of it. Work the position through in the
scratchpad: read the board, name candidate lines, check each for blockers, count free
cells, and say where every card you care about is. Keep going in the scratchpad until you
have actually settled on a line.

The scratchpad is unlimited and is the right place for long reasoning. After the
scratchpad you must call `play` with the moves you settled on. If you need to reconsider
after a tool result, call `scratchpad` again before playing.
